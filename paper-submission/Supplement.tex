This supplement reports the supporting studies that the manuscript summarizes in
Section~VII-A and the full served-model results of Section~VII-E. All values are regenerated
from the released artifact bundle. Results other than Table~X use the deterministic generator
and adversary $\mathcal{U}$, the unauthorized writer.

Table~V lists the evaluation corpora. Fig.~4 shows attack success for every configuration and
attack, and Fig.~5 and Table~VIII the ingestion-control ladder. Table~VI removes one evasion
technique at a time from the rewriting adversary. Table~VII gives the retrieval filter's
detection quality. Table~IX separates the contribution of generator susceptibility. Table~X
gives the seven-configuration served-model results for Qwen2.5~7B and Llama~3.1~8B on 100
SQuAD questions with one seed.
